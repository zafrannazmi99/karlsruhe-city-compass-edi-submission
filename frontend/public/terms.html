Terms of Use

Karlsruhe City Compass is an educational project and local place-discovery demo. By using it, you agree to use it lawfully and understand that the content is provided for general information.

Place information and routes

Place records may be incomplete, outdated, or incorrectly mapped. A live check confirms that an OpenStreetMap record exists and still matches its mapped category; it cannot confirm that a venue is physically present or open. If the live map service is unavailable, a recent local snapshot may be shown and is labelled as not live-verified. Walking routes are estimates from mapped paths; when that service is unavailable, the map clearly falls back to straight-line estimates. Confirm hours, access rules, and suitability with the linked source before visiting. Do not use the app for emergencies or safety-critical decisions.

Third-party services

The app uses OpenStreetMap place data and walking routes, CARTO map tiles, and Photon geocoding. These services are subject to their respective terms and attribution requirements. OpenStreetMap data is available under the Open Database License.

Availability

This demo is provided as-is and may be unavailable or return incomplete recommendations. No promise is made that any result is currently open or accepting visitors.
